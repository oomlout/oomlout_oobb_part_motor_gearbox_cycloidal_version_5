Print these 3dpr samples using the intended material and layer height.
6703 sample: check the central bearing fits and rests on its outer-race lip.
The adjacent guide orbit hole is 6.20 mm for a 3 mm smooth pin, 1.4 mm orbit,
and 0.2 mm radial clearance. It is deliberately not an M3 fit hole.
6701 sample: check the replacement front shaft bearing seat and supporting lip.
6810 ring: print open seat up; test the bearing fit and outer-race stop.
Split casing samples: clamp their bed faces together with an M3x6 countersunk screw and M3 nut. Load the nut from the outside of the lower seam flange.
The clamp samples use the actual three-M6/two-M3 tab pattern. Flip the lower sample so its captive nut aligns with the upper countersink.
OOBB tab sample: print flat with no slicer support; check its M6/M3 through-holes before printing the full casings.
Direct-drive sample, if present: load the M3 nut from underneath; insert an M3x8 grub screw from the side.
Test on the actual 5 mm motor shaft. The screw presses on the shaft; avoid crushing the printed nut pocket.
Standoff samples, if present: fit the socket over the peg until its face sits on the shoulder; fasten with an M3x25 socket screw and bottom-loaded M3 nut.
There are no guide bearings or split-disc screws. Prototype loading and wear remain untested.
