# Outer housing reprint

Print [3dpr.stl](3dpr.stl) once for the two square plates and both bearing cylinders. To use the lower-row shape, substitute one [lower-row plate](../native_parts/output_lower_row_plate.stl) for each square plate you want to replace. All bearing pockets face upwards in the supplied print orientations.

Place a 6810 into each cylinder. Close each with a flat plate against the exposed outer race. Put the two plate faces against the ring spacers and align their lobe holes. Pass 20 M3 × 30 socket-cap screws from the cylinder with round recesses into the nuts in the opposite cylinder. The lobe screws clamp the whole assembly.
