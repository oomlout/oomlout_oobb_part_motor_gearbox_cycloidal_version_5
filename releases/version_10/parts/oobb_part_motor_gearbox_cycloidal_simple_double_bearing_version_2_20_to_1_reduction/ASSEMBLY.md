# Gearbox assembly

AI-generated images and instructions. Errors possible.

## 1. Prepare both cycloids

Capture one 6704 between each disc's halves. Fit three M3 nuts and three M3 x 6 countersunk screws per disc; seat the heads flush before stacking.

![AI-generated CAD illustration: prepare both cycloids](../../presentation/images/step-01.png)

## 2. Stand the screws upward

Start with output_cap_cylinder, carrier_top_cap and yellow shaft_end_upper (E), flanges down. Fit 20 M3 x 30 ring screws, six M3 x 35 guides and two M3 x 35 shaft screws from underneath. One shaft head temporarily occupies E's empty hex pocket.

![AI-generated CAD illustration: stand the screws upward](../../presentation/images/step-02.png)

## 3. Seat the starting bearings

Lower a 6810 over the large black journal and a centred 6704 over yellow E. Seat both on their shoulders.

![AI-generated CAD illustration: seat the starting bearings](../../presentation/images/step-03.png)

## 4. Close the starting layer

Load eight M3 nuts into carrier_top and four into shaft_mount_upper (N). Retain the nuts with their pockets downward; lower the core, N and output_cap_plate. Support the plate with at least 50 mm access underneath.

![AI-generated CAD illustration: close the starting layer](../../presentation/images/step-04.png)

## 5. Fit the sleeves

Slide six guide sleeves onto the inner screws and twenty contact sleeves onto the outer screws. The connected ring replaces the twenty outer sleeves only.

![AI-generated CAD illustration: fit the sleeves](../../presentation/images/step-05.png)

## 6. Fit the first cam and Disc B

Lower shaft_cam_b_outer with its narrow journal up. Lower the closed Disc B with its screw heads down and its indexed clamp screw toward the wide guide gap.

![AI-generated CAD illustration: fit the first cam and disc b](../../presentation/images/step-06.png)

## 7. Close B and start A

Fit shaft_cam_b_inner with its journal down, then shaft_cam_a_inner with its journal up. Set A's eccentric offset 180 degrees opposite B.

![AI-generated CAD illustration: close b and start a](../../presentation/images/step-07.png)

## 8. Fit Disc A and close its cam

Lower Disc A with its heads up and index toward the same wide guide gap. Seat its bearing, then fit shaft_cam_a_outer with its journal down.

![AI-generated CAD illustration: fit disc a and close its cam](../../presentation/images/step-08.png)

## 9. Close the finishing inner layer

Load eight nuts into carrier_bottom and four into shaft_mount_lower (N), pockets upward. Lower those pieces and output_nut_plate; seat the guide sleeves in the core sockets.

![AI-generated CAD illustration: close the finishing inner layer](../../presentation/images/step-09.png)

## 10. Seat the finishing bearings

Lower the remaining 6810 onto the outer plate and carrier shoulder. Lower the remaining centred 6704 onto N.

![AI-generated CAD illustration: seat the finishing bearings](../../presentation/images/step-10.png)

## 11. Fit the finishing caps

Lower output_nut_cylinder, carrier_bottom_cap and shaft_end_lower (E), flanges up. Match E's round socket to the temporary screw and its hex pocket to the retained screw.

![AI-generated CAD illustration: fit the finishing caps](../../presentation/images/step-11.png)

## 12. Reverse the temporary shaft screw

Secure the correctly directed shaft tie and start the ring and guide nuts. Withdraw the temporary screw underneath. Load the vacated hex pocket with an M3 nut; reverse the same screw and insert it from above.

![AI-generated CAD illustration: reverse the temporary shaft screw](../../presentation/images/step-12.png)

## 13. Adjust the fasteners

Snug the opposed shaft ties, then the six guides, then the twenty ring screws evenly. Hold the teal outer ring fixed and turn E by hand after each group. The black middle ring should turn freely. Reseat any bearing, sleeve or nut that binds.

![AI-generated CAD illustration: adjust the fasteners](../../presentation/images/step-13.png)

## 14. Install the gearbox

Fix the teal outer ring through its OOBB mounting plates. Couple the drive to E; its face is recessed 0.8 mm behind the black middle ring. Attach the load to the black middle ring through its captive M3 mounting points. Keep the 6.50 mm centre passage clear and select mounting screws for the attached parts.

![AI-generated CAD illustration: install the gearbox](../../presentation/images/step-14.png)
