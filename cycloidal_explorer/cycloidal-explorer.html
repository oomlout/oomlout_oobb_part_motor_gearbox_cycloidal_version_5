WHAT V5 TAUGHT US

01 / RATIO

Count the rotating member.

Here, N contacts give an N:1 casing reduction and N−1 disc lobes. A conventional fixed-ring, rotating-disc design instead gives (N−1):1 in reverse. This explorer models the project's casing output.

02 / CONTACTS

Larger isn't always smoother.

The old R = 30.15, e = 1.4, r = 3 combination undercuts. V5 uses R = 31.15. “Lobe radius” means the circular ring-contact radius, not the disc's outer radius. The contacts are printed features; M3 is an axle size, not a roller diameter.

03 / PACKAGING

Keep the bearing in context.

Two 6810 bearings establish a Ø65 package. Orbit holes grow by twice the eccentricity. The current V5 is Ø72.3 × 32 mm above the motor face, with a 74 mm mounting plate; changed variants need their axial stack recalculated.

Equations, assumptions and source trace

Ported from gearbox_native/cycloid_equation.py, radial sizing in captured.py, simple.py, interfaces.py, and the active options in working_oomp_populate.py. The undercut regression is in tests/test_native_three_mm_lobes.py.

q(t) = [R cos(t) − e cos(Nt), R sin(t) − e sin(Nt)]
u = R cos(t) − Ne cos(Nt); v = R sin(t) − Ne sin(Nt)
profile = q − (r + clearance) [u,v] / √(u² + v²)
guide hole Ø = 2 × static screw radius + 2e + 0.40
standoff hole Ø = 2 × standoff radius + 2e + 0.30
automatic R = round_up_0.05(minimum_R_under_V5_rules + radius_buffer)
casing Ø = 2 max(R + r + wall, 32.5 + 0.10 + wall)

Default V5 interfaces: six Ø3 guides on Ø34.8 PCD, three Ø5.4 standoffs on Ø37.1 PCD, and two 6703 centre bearings. Body radii, position radii and centre bearings are now editable. Internal web target remains 2 mm and the retained casing-lip aperture is Ø62.4. The minimum pitch radius retains the V5 0.90 coefficient limit, 2 mm feature webs, contact spacing and analytic undercut check. It is the smallest allowed 0.05 mm step under these rules, not an unrestricted optimisation of the complete assembly. A low reduction may be limited by the fixed holes rather than the ratio. The buffer enlarges the pitch radius; it does not change hole spacing, bearing choices or clearances. Too much buffer can exceed the fixed 6810 insertion lip. The displayed disc diameters are sampled about its own centre; the swept envelope adds its orbit. The bottom disc is offset 180° in eccentric phase and −180°/(N−1) in profile phase. The dashed pitch circle and the bearing are reference geometry.

No load, torque, backlash, material, lubrication or service-life prediction is made. The 24.5 mm baseline bearing/disc stack excludes carriers, shoulders, clearances and motor interfaces; it is not overall gearbox height. The current split casing prints split-face down. Guide surfaces and fit coupons still need physical verification.

Bearing catalogue sources and widths

The bundled reference menu covers 6700–6711 and 6800–6812, with independent top and bottom centre-bearing menus and a None option. ENRO 6700–6706, Yongnuo 6700-series table, and NSK catalogue, B-15 supply nominal dimensions. The menu distinguishes 6700 (10 × 15 × 4) from JTEKT open 6700 (10 × 15 × 3); use the width of your actual bearing. Reference selection does not redesign the casing. The native update supports independent centre bearings when the bearing seats, journals and assembly clearances fit. None and special-width study bearings are not production selections.
